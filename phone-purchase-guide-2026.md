## 5. Rejected models — one-line reasons

- **Moto G06 Power** — Helio G81 (slower than F23's chip), 6.88" too big, 3.7★
- **iQOO Z10 Lite** — HD+ LCD, D6300 downgrade, ~2 OS updates
- **Samsung M07** — 4G-only, 4GB RAM, HD+; M17e dominates it at this budget
- **Nothing Phone 2a** — 2024 launch, discontinued, >half its update life consumed
- **Moto Edge 50 Neo** — 2024 launch, support ends ~2029, small battery; Edge 60 Fusion replaced it
- **OnePlus Nord CE4** — Android updates finished, security ends Apr 2027, still ₹21–25k
- **iQOO Z9s Pro** — support ends ~2027, price hasn't crashed; Z10R supersedes it (but ~2 OS updates → weak long-hold)
- **Moto G96** — good hardware but weakest update promise (1–3 OS); wrong trade for 5-year hold

## 7. Sale-day checklist

- [ ] Compare **final cart price** (sale + bank offer + exchange + coupon) on Amazon AND Flipkart — bank offers swing ₹1–1.5k
- [ ] Buy **6GB/128GB minimum**; 8GB if Δ ≤ ₹1k; 256GB only if Δ ≤ ₹1.5k (else use SD card)
- [ ] Confirm listing states the update promise ("6 OS upgrades" etc.)
- [ ] All shortlisted phones ship charger in box (Samsung 25W, POCO 45W, Moto 68W) — verify on listing
- [ ] Within 7–10 day return window: test low-light camera, both SIM slots, speaker, network bands
- [ ] Avoid discounted **2024 stock** (Nothing 2a, Edge 50 Neo, CE4, Z9s Pro) — the discount is their expired update life
- [ ] F23 exchange bonus is higher during sales, but base value is modest — compare with private resale
