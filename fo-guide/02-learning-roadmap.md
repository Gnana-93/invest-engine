# Guide 02 — Learning Roadmap & Curated Resources

> Built for: 1 hour study/day (evening or morning) + 10–15 min market-hours glance.
> Full mix: official curriculum + videos + books + daily practice.

| # | Resource | What | Cost | Use it for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Zerodha Varsity — Options module** (zerodha.com/varsity) | ~14-chapter options theory + strategies module | Free (+ optional certification) | Your **main textbook**. Read 1–2 chapters/day |

| 4 | **NSE India website** | Contract specs, circulars, holiday list | Free | Verification of lot sizes/expiry rules |

**Supplementary (pick, don't swallow):**

- **YouTube:** stick to exchange/broker-run content (Varsity videos, Sensibull channel). Avoid any channel that shows "P&L screenshots" and sells courses/telegram groups. Red flag test: *"Does this person show a verifiable track record or teach SEBI-style risk first?"* If not — skip.
- **Books (later, month 2+):** NISM Series VIII workbook (equity derivatives — most structured Indian text, also prepares you if you ever want certification), then Sheldon Natenberg *"Option Volatility & Pricing"* (the classic, read after 2 months of paper trading).
- **Certification (optional):** NISM Series VIII is not needed to trade, but its syllabus is a great checklist. Do it only if you enjoy structured exams.

## 2. The 4-week curriculum (matches your paper trading plan)

### Week 1 — Mechanics (options = contracts)
- **Varsity:** Options module chapters 1–6 (what are options, calls, puts, moneyness, premium).
- **Sensibull videos:** "Options basics" track.
- **Practice:** open Sensibull virtual mode; just *read* the Nifty option chain daily: spot price, ATM strike, CE/PE premiums, bid-ask spreads. Log 3 observations/day in your journal.
- **Milestone:** explain to yourself why an ITM call costs more than an OTM call (Guide 01 §3).

### Week 2 — The engine room (Greeks & volatility)
- **Varsity:** options theory module — Greeks (delta/theta/gamma/vega) + volatility chapters.
- **Sensibull:** Greeks video track; open the strategy builder and *watch* how theta shows per strategy.
- **Practice:** pick one ATM and one OTM Nifty weekly; note their premiums at 10:00 and 15:00 daily. Compute the decay. Feel theta.
- **Milestone:** predict (roughly) which loses more by Tuesday: ATM or deep-OTM weekly, and why.

### Week 3 — Strategies with defined risk
- **Varsity:** strategies module — covered call, protective put, bull-put spread (credit), bear-call spread, iron condor overview.
- **Practice (paper):** place your first **defined-risk paper trades** per Guide 04's schedule: one long call/put (small premium), one bull-put spread, one covered call on a stock you hold (virtual).
- **Milestone:** for each trade, state max profit, max loss, breakeven *before* placing it.

### Week 4 — Management, costs, tax, review
- **Varsity:** position management + taxation chapters; read Zerodha charges page fully.
- **Practice:** manage your open paper trades: when do you book/roll? Enter with 50% of normal size. Track costs as if real (Guide 01 §7).
- **Milestone:** complete journal review — win rate, average win/loss, biggest mistake. This decides your go/no-go (Guide 06).

## 3. Daily routine template (fits your 1 hour)

**Evening (45–50 min), the heavy lift:**
1. 15 min — read current Varsity chapter(s) (take 5 bullet notes).
2. 10 min — corresponding Sensibull video if the chapter was hard.
3. 15 min — journal: today's paper trades, plan for tomorrow (orders to place, with strikes + max loss written down).
4. 5–10 min — Varsity quiz for the chapter.

**Market hours (10–15 min, mid-day):**
- Open Sensibull → check your open virtual positions → nothing else. No trading decisions mid-day in week 1–2; place planned orders at 9:20–9:30 or 14:45–15:15 only (decided the previous evening).

> Discipline rule: **the evening plans, the market executes.** If you find yourself
> placing unplanned mid-day trades, note it in the journal — that's your first
> behavioural finding, and it's gold.

## 4. What we deliberately skip (for now)

- Futures (leverage, MTM, rollovers) — revisit after 3 months of profitable defined-risk trading.
- Intraday/weekly scalping — incompatible with your 10–15 min availability.
- Naked selling, iron condors with 4 legs, delta-neutral hedging — month 2+ topics.
- Crypto/US-options analogies from YouTube — Indian market structure is different (lot sizes, physical settlement, STT).

---

## 5. Progress self-audit (end of each week, 10 min)

Score yourself 0/1/2 on: concepts understood · journal maintained · planned-trade discipline · ability to state max loss on every position · quiz pass rate.

- **12–14:** on track.
- **8–11:** repeat the weakest week's material; delay paper schedule by one week (that's fine — milestones over calendar).
- **< 8:** slow down before Guide 04; do Week 1–2 material twice.
