# Guide 06 — Going Live: Transition Checklist, Risk Rules & Milestone Gates

> Read AFTER completing the 4-week paper plan. Do not read casually before.
> **The 3–4 week timeline is a minimum, not a promise.** Milestones gate you, not dates.

---

## 1. The go-live gate (pass ALL, or stay in paper)

**A. Process gates (from Guide 04 scoring):**
- [ ] 6–8 paper trades completed, 100% journaled
- [ ] ≥80% of exits executed exactly per pre-written plan
- [ ] ₹10,000 max-loss cap never breached
- [ ] Mistake Counter ≤ 3
- [ ] Weeks 1–3 exit checklists all passed

**B. Knowledge gates:**
- [ ] Can state max profit/loss/breakeven for covered call, CSP, bull put, bear call in 30 seconds each
- [ ] Can explain why you will never sell naked options or trade weekly-expiry-day shorts
- [ ] Know your costs per round trip (Guide 01 §7) without looking

**C. Life gates:**
- [ ] Live capital is money you can lose 100% of without changing your lifestyle
- [ ] You accept: first 6 live months are tuition, not income
- [ ] Family/spouse knows and agrees with the risk envelope

**Failed a gate? Repeat the relevant paper week. That's the system working, not failing.**

---

## 2. Phased live ramp (12 weeks minimum)

| Phase | Duration | Capital | Allowed strategies | Success criterion to advance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Weeks 1–2 | **1 lot only, ₹10–15k risk** | Long options only (ATM/monthly) | Followed every planned exit; losses ≤ 2 caps |
| 1 | Weeks 3–6 | 1 lot | + Bull put spread (half size from Guide 05) | 4+ spreads; early-exit rule followed when triggered |
| 2 | Weeks 7–10 | 1–2 lots | + Bear call spread; + covered call on ONE holding | Quarter-to-date net ≥ −5% (yes, still loss-tolerant) |
| 3 | Week 11+ | 2–3 lots max | Full Guide 05 menu + collar experiments | — |

**The 6-month honesty rule:** judge yourself on 6-month cumulative P&L vs costs, not any
single week. Income strategies look boring most weeks and bad some months. That's the shape
of the curve — it's supposed to look like that.

---

## 3. Hard risk rules (non-negotiable, print this)

1. **Max loss per idea: 2% of live capital.** One lot spreads only until Phase 3.
2. **Max open ideas: 2.** Correlated positions (2 index spreads) count as ONE idea.
3. **Never add to a losing spread.** Averaging into short premium is how 88% became 88%.
4. **No new positions:** expiry day, result days for your stocks, RBI/Budget days.
5. **Early-exit rule (from Guide 05) executes mechanically.** No "let me wait one more day" — that one day is where ₹9,750 losses live.
6. **Drawdown circuit-breaker:** if live account is down **10% from starting capital → stop, 2-week paper review, restart Phase 1 if resumed.**
7. **Weekly limit on screen-fiddling:** your 10–15 min window is for *checking*, and evenings for *deciding*. If mid-day trades sneak in, your Mistake Counter — even if they profit — because the process broke.

---

## 4. Operational go-live setup (one evening)

- [ ] Activate derivatives segment in Zerodha (Console → account → segment activation; needs income proof typically)
- [ ] Margins pledge setup if you plan covered calls on holdings (understand margin shortfall risk first)
- [ ] Kite mobile: F&O order window practice (limit orders ONLY; no market orders in options)
- [ ] Sensibull switched to *live tracking* mode; keep Draft Portfolio for parallel paper testing
- [ ] Same journal, new tab: "LIVE" — every live trade mirrored in a "shadow paper trade" row for the first 10 trades (measures your emotion gap)
- [ ] GCal/timer for your 13:00 check window + evening review block

---

## 5. The first 10 live trades (expectations)

- Target is **not** profit. Target: process adherence 100%, emotional gap measured.
- The shadow-paper comparison tells you the "emotion tax": live result vs identical paper
  instruction. Most people discover they exit winners too early and losers too late.
  Seeing your own number is the single most valuable go-live output.
- Expected outcome distribution for a prepared beginner: ~5–7 small wins/losses, 2–3
  rule-breach temptations caught, 1–2 genuine mistakes with small cost. Total P&L ±₹5k.
  All fine. Tuition paid cheap.

---

## 6. Kill-switch criteria (stop immediately, reassess from paper)

- Two consecutive months of losses > 5% each
- Any trade exceeding the 2% cap "because conviction"
- Journal abandoned for 2+ weeks (no journal = no trading, this is rule zero)
- You're checking prices outside your windows (compulsion signature)

Hitting any → back to paper + Guide 02 Week 2 material + honest review. The account can be
rebuilt; the habit of breaking rules cannot be un-built, only interrupted.

---

## 7. Beyond month 3 (once consistently process-clean)

- Add position sizing theory (fixed-fractional), IV percentile screens for entry timing
- Study roll mechanics for covered calls (avoid assignment around dividends)
- Learn collar/PMCC structures for larger capital efficiency
- Optional: NISM Series VIII certification for structured depth
- Then — and only then — revisit whether futures fit your system (they still probably don't, given your time budget, and that's okay)
